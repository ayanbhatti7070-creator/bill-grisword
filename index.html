04 / About

Current About / summary, as supplied

Established in 1994, First Choice Business Brokers has grown to become one of the largest organizations in the U.S. specializing in business sales. We have listed and managed the sale of over $7.5 billion in businesses for sale. Buyers and sellers alike can enjoy the accessibility and expertise of a national network of professional business brokers and the largest inventory of businesses available for sale. After growing up in a military family and finally settling in the Hampton Roads area, William (Bill) Griswold started his career in retail sales. That new beginning led to him purchasing his first business while in college. Through time, dedication and Bill’s leadership, his company developed into one of the largest stainless-steel manufacturing companies on the east coast. After selling it and moving to the Richmond area and marrying his wife Patricia. Bill then started working with a handful of Fortune 500 companies; Motorola, Security Link and ADT in their commercial sales departments. Having these experiences sparked several successful business ventures for Bill, including graphic design, insurance brokerage and tax preparation businesses, some they still run today. Through having experiences in multiple facets of business, Bill has developed a keen understanding of the opportunities and challenges that come with buying and selling companies. Through first-hand, seasoned comprehension of the business world, Bill knows how important it is to have the knowledge, professionalism and confidentiality that First Choice Business Brokers brings to the table.

Proposed About section

Buying or selling a business is more than a transaction. It is a decision tied to years of work, financial goals, employees, family, and what comes next. I work with business owners exploring a sale and buyers looking for the right business opportunity through First Choice Business Brokers in Virginia. I bring firsthand experience as a business owner to these conversations. I purchased my first business while in college and later built a stainless-steel manufacturing company that grew into one of the largest of its kind on the East Coast. After selling that business, I worked in commercial sales with companies including Motorola, Security Link, and ADT, and went on to be involved in other business ventures. That path gave me an appreciation for the practical realities behind business decisions: understanding value, preparing for a transition, finding the right fit, and handling sensitive information with care. Whether you are considering selling a business, exploring what it may be worth, or searching for an acquisition, the first step is a thoughtful conversation about your goals and options. I believe business transitions deserve professionalism, clear communication, and confidentiality. If a sale or acquisition is on your horizon in Virginia, feel free to connect or send me a message to start the conversation.
